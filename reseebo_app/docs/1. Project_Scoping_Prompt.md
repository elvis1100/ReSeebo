## 1. Project Overview

- **App Name:** Reseebo
- **One-sentence Pitch:** Help sari-sari store owners scan purchase receipts, compare the same products across suppliers and dates, and plan purchases and selling prices offline.
- **Hackathon Theme/Topic Alignment:** Meaningful receipt recognition runs on the phone using a bundled pretrained model. First launch, scanning, review, local saving, comparisons, and calculations must work with internet disabled immediately after installation.

## 2. Core Features (The Most Viable Product - MVP)

*List the absolutely critical 2-3 features. Everything else is out of scope.*

1. **Scan and confirm:** Capture/import a receipt, extract draft information locally, correct missing details, confirm product matches, and save the purchase.
2. **Compare prices:** Show dated tables for the same brand, variant, and size across suppliers and over time at each supplier. Preserve all purchases, including same-day purchases.
3. **Plan and calculate:** On the product comparison screen, show suggested selling prices using the owner's markup settings. Optional restock quantity and pricing controls calculate estimated costs, savings, and gross profit. Group buying suggestions by supplier and show any extra travel/delivery costs separately.

All three flows run on the phone. Accounts, cloud backup, and online synchronization are outside the current scope.

## 3. Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter with Dart for the mobile app. Keep screens, state, models, and services separate.
- **Application logic:** On-device Dart services for receipt parsing, confirmed product matching, comparisons, and calculations. No hosted backend or cloud AI calls.
- **Database:** On-device SQLite, suggested package `sqflite`, for persistent records and everyday reads/writes. Store receipt images, product photos, and the optional store photo/logo in persistent app-local files.
- **Key APIs / Integrations:** Suggested `google_mlkit_text_recognition` for native on-device OCR, configured with bundled models; `image_picker` for camera/gallery; `path_provider` for persistent local image files. Receipt parsing, confirmed product matching, and calculations run in Dart. OCR does not automatically identify every receipt field.

Package references: [OCR](https://pub.dev/packages/google_mlkit_text_recognition) (`google_mlkit_text_recognition: ^0.14.0`), [bundled models](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/tips/installation-paths) (bundled offline via Android metadata `com.google.mlkit.vision.DEPENDENCIES`), [SQLite](https://docs.flutter.dev/cookbook/persistence/sqlite) (`sqflite: ^2.3.0`), [image capture](https://pub.dev/packages/image_picker) (`image_picker: ^1.1.2`), [local files](https://pub.dev/packages/path_provider) (`path_provider: ^2.1.4`). Verified baseline: Flutter 3.24+ / Dart 3.5+. Bundle required models, fonts, and assets; runtime downloads must not be needed.

## 4. Target Users

- **Who are they?** Philippine sari-sari store owners buying stock from groceries, wholesalers, or other suppliers.
- **What is their main pain point?** Manually comparing old receipts, abbreviated product names, and pack prices is time-consuming. Prices may rise on the next purchase, even at the same supplier, reducing tubo if selling prices stay unchanged.

## 5. Design & Vibe

- **Color Palette / Theme:** "Palengke Green & Clean Ledger". Deep Emerald (`#0F5132`) primary, Warm Amber (`#D97706`) for unconfirmed/draft alerts, and Slate neutrals (`#F8FAFC` surface, `#0F172A` text). Dual-encoded price trends (green `↓` for savings, red `↑` for increases).
- **Inspiration (URL/App):** Peddlr / Packworks (utilitarian sari-sari POS layout), Maya transaction history (high-contrast financial ledgers), and document scanners (split-screen receipt inspection).
- **Primary Typography:** Bundled `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` for offline use. Strict tabular numbers (`FontFeature.tabularFigures()`) for aligned peso amounts (`₱`) and pack units; high-contrast 16px body for fast counter glanceability.

## 6. Data Entities (Rough Draft)

*List the main things your app needs to store. Fields and relationships are provisional; refine the schema during backend implementation.*

- **Store profile:** One local profile for the owner's sari-sari store: ID, store name, optional owner name, optional local photo/logo reference, and owner-selected default markup percentage. No account or login; multiple store workspaces are outside the MVP.
- **Products:** Reusable product ID, name, brand, variant, size/unit, optional category, optional barcode, one optional local photo reference, optional markup percentage override, and optional owner-set selling price per piece. Confirm receipt descriptions against this catalog; different variants/sizes stay separate.
- **Product aliases:** Alias ID, product ID, and confirmed receipt description. Reuse owner-confirmed mappings to suggest matches on later scans; ambiguous descriptions require confirmation and must not silently merge products.
- **Suppliers:** Reusable supplier ID, confirmed name, optional branch/location, and notes. Keep distinct branches identifiable.
- **Receipts:** Receipt ID, supplier ID, purchase date/time when available, local image reference, recorded timestamp, optional receipt number, and notes. Receipt numbers assist duplicate review; they are not globally unique identifiers.
- **Purchase lines:** Line ID, receipt ID, product ID, original description, purchased quantity/unit, pieces per pack, confirmed net line amount, and calculated cost per piece. Each purchase adds history; prices belong to purchases rather than overwriting the product record.

These are six provisional tables. Add created/updated timestamps to each entity; keep purchase dates distinct from edit timestamps. Copy chosen photos into persistent app storage and save local file references in SQLite. Photos are optional and use placeholders when absent.

Pricing settings are stored; suggestions are calculated from the purchase cost being compared. Use a product's markup override when set (including 0%), otherwise the store default. Ask the owner to choose a default markup; do not assume one. Show an owner-set selling price separately from the calculated suggestion and calculate estimated gross profit against the selected purchase cost. Editing profile, product, or pricing settings must not rewrite earlier purchase quantities or costs.

## 7. First MVP User Journey

- **Starting point:** Open the installed app offline, with a few earlier purchases already saved from real receipts.
- **User actions:** Scan/import another receipt → review fields → confirm product matches → save → compare supplier prices and suggested selling prices.
- **Optional actions:** Edit the local store profile or product details/photos; enter a restock quantity or adjust markup/selling price on the comparison screen.
- **Successful result:** A saved receipt survives app restart; comparison tables show supplier, purchase date, comparable unit cost, and changes. Planning shows dated price-based suggestions and estimated costs/gross profit, with unknown prices visible.
- **Out of scope:** Accounts, cloud backup, online synchronization, full inventory/sales tracking, demand forecasting, live supplier prices, ordering, cross-brand substitutions, and required generated summaries. Expiry tracking remains a discussion item. Final branding is "Reseebo: Tindahan Price & Tubo Tracker"; demo device is a physical Android phone running Android 10+ (API 29+) tested in Airplane mode.

## 8. Initial Integration Plan

*Fill in only what is known for the first MVP journey. Unknown details can remain TBD; no full schema or ongoing Markdown updates are required.*

- **Connection approach:** Flutter calls local OCR, parsing, database, and calculation services through a data adapter. All app operations use on-device data and processing.
- **Authentication / access:** No account or sign-in. Store receipts and images in app-local storage.
- **First operations:** Read/update the local store profile and product details/photos/pricing settings; extract a receipt draft; confirm product aliases; save a confirmed receipt and lines; list products/receipts; retrieve supplier comparisons and history; calculate restock/pricing scenarios.
- **Expected input / result:** Image → editable `ReceiptDraft` with text bounds and `ParsedLineItem` rows. Confirmed draft → `SaveReceiptResult` with saved receipt/line IDs and unit costs. Product ID → `ProductComparisonData` with dated `SupplierPriceRow` and `PriceHistoryRecord` entries. Restock quantities or selling price → `RestockEstimate` and `ProfitCalculation`. Concrete contracts established in `IReseeboDataAdapter`.
- **Expected failures:** Camera denied/capture canceled, unreadable image, ambiguous product or pack quantity, invalid amounts, duplicate-save attempts, and local storage failure. Keep drafts editable and local data intact.
- **First integration check:** Install the app, disable internet before first launch, set a store name/default markup, add a product photo, scan a real receipt, correct and save it, restart the app, then compare with an earlier purchase and check suggested selling prices. Optionally enter a restock quantity or selling price. Verify profile, photos, confirmed aliases, pricing settings, receipts, and original images persist offline.

Agree on missing interface details before either side depends on them. Match mocks to the agreed inputs/results, then connect one real journey early. Revisit this document only for material scope changes.
